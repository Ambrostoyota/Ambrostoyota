## The opportunity

Support an independently operated, transparent research program studying **when autonomous open-weight AI agents actually work**, where they fail, and what hardware resources they need. The planned public outputs are benchmark protocols, reproducible experiments, a prototype, and detailed technical reports.

This is a **research-infrastructure sponsorship**, not an investment security, charitable donation claim, equity offering, or purchase of exclusive intellectual property.

### What the funding enables

Primary planned resource: **Apple Mac Studio with M5 Ultra and 512GB unified memory**, contingent on actual final quote, availability, research-fit evaluation, and sponsor agreement. This is intended for **local inference, multi-model orchestration, evaluation and suitably sized fine-tuning**—not frontier-model pretraining.

Apple's August 2026 announcement states that the 512GB configuration is expected in late October; the published **M5 Ultra starting price is not the 512GB configured price**. See [Apple's announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-new-mac-studio-with-m5-max-and-m5-ultra/).

1. Confirm actual configured quote and tax before spending.
2. Track requests, commitments, receipts and expenses separately.
3. If costs exceed funds, pause, disclose and revise the plan; no sponsor owes overruns.
4. Disclose material changes to hardware specification, project scope, and transfers exceeding $500 between categories.
