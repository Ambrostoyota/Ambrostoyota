# Sponsor diligence FAQ

### Is the lab established?

This is a proposed independent, 12-month research program. The research agenda and funding request are published, but a funded laboratory and benchmark outcomes should not be assumed to exist.

### Is the $18,000 amount a manufacturer quotation?

No. It is a planning target. Apple's M5 Ultra **base starting price** is different from the price of a specific 512GB configuration. Procurement requires a dated quote and tax calculation first.

### Why local inference rather than only renting GPUs?

Local hardware could make iterative testing predictable and improve data control. The project will evaluate this hypothesis against cloud pricing, practical throughput, memory use, energy consumption and model compatibility. The hardware choice may be revised if evidence favors alternatives.

### Can I sponsor less than $3,000?

The formal campaign models six $3,000 founding slots. Smaller contributions, shared hardware, hardware discounts or research credits can be discussed, but no payment route or sponsor tier should be advertised until available and approved.

### Is sponsorship tax deductible? Do I get equity?

Neither is promised. This is an independent effort with no claimed nonprofit status or university affiliation. Sponsorship does not imply stock, debt repayment, profit sharing, or exclusive commercial rights.

### What if the campaign is only partially funded?

Procurement will be delayed or the plan revised and presented to sponsors. Written terms—including refunds or alternatives when appropriate—must be agreed **before accepting money**. The lead will not automatically spend partial funds on another purpose.

### What if the chosen Mac configuration is unavailable or over budget?

Provide verified alternatives, costs and technical trade-offs. Explain material changes and secure any approvals required by each written agreement before spending.

### How will sponsors verify progress?

Quarterly research and expenditure updates; versioned reports with run metadata; code and protocols publicly linked where licensing, safety and privacy permit. Negative findings will not be hidden.

### Will sponsors control technical findings?

No. Researchers retain responsibility for method, reporting, and research integrity. Funders may receive acknowledgment but not favorable results or publication vetoes.

### Can sponsors have private access or product rights?

Any proprietary services, priority engineering support, intellectual-property licensing, or commissioned work would require a **separate signed agreement** and is not part of the founding sponsorship offer.

### How can I review the evidence?

Start with the [research agenda](../research/RESEARCH_AGENDA.md), [benchmark protocol](../research/BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md) and [evidence register](../research/EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md). Links to completed results will be added only after verification.
